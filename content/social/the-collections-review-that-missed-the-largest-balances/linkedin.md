At Triten, historical collections questions could return a later balance or an incorrect zero. Tenants with extensive charge histories were also being excluded from aging results.[^E1][^E2][^E4]

I corrected how balance dates were represented, added answers from the source system’s month-end ledger and aligned historical property associations with the ledger date.[^E1][^E2][^E3]

For aging, I tied the supporting evidence to each age band’s own charges. That allowed tenants excluded because of their combined histories to appear.[^E4]

I also added an independent, read-only source comparison. Reconciliation matched all current-tenant charges and former-tenant balances checked. Some former-tenant age-band allocations still differed.[^E3][^E5]

A remaining limit can still exclude a tenant when an individual age band has too much supporting evidence. The evidence is never truncated.[^E4]

Those are the boundaries of the result: corrected historical answers, broader aging coverage and specific exceptions still requiring attention.[^E1][^E2][^E3][^E4][^E5]
