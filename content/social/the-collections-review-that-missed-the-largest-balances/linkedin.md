Every aging band carried all of its tenant’s open charges as supporting evidence. When that exceeded the evidence limit, the entire tenant was excluded.[^E4]

I changed the rule so each band cites the tenant and the charges allocated to that band.[^E4]

The remaining boundary matters: an individual band with too many supporting records still excludes the tenant.[^E4] The supplied record gives no verified restoration count or associated balance total.[^E4]

The accounting checks have their own results. Month-end balances matched for 203 of 209 tenants.[^E1] Current-tenant charges matched for 990 of 990 charges checked. Former-tenant balances matched for 43 of 43 tenants, while aging allocations matched for 31 of 43.[^E5]

Those comparisons document accounting agreement. The recovery count remains unverified.[^E4]
