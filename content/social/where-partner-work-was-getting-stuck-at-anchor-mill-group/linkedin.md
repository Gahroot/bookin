An assignment could be dispatched without a portal account. The response clock started, but the notification was never sent.

I added password-setting invitations and blocked dispatch when the partner lacked an account. Staff also get confirmation of whether invitation delivery succeeded.

The submission handoff needed attention too. Bulk uploads now notify the coordinator and start review tracking for each deliverable.

For revisions, reviewers must give itemized feedback. Partners respond point by point, and resubmissions retain the connection to the previous version and the request they answer.

The completion rule now accounts for final rejections: every non-rejected item must be approved, and an approved item must exist. Entirely rejected assignments remain with the coordinator.

This work addressed the gaps between assigning work, receiving it and deciding it was complete.
