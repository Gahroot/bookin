Dispatch started the response clock even when the partner had no portal account and no notification was sent.

I made an account a condition of dispatch. Without it, the assignment stays in draft and staff receive an actionable warning.

The next handoff was submission. Bulk uploads now notify the coordinator and start review tracking for each deliverable. Revision requests require itemized feedback, and partners answer each point when resubmitting.

Completion needed a more deliberate rule. Final rejections could keep otherwise approved assignments open.

I changed the rule so every non-rejected deliverable must be approved, with at least one approved deliverable required. The completion check also runs after a rejection.

Assignments containing only rejected work remain with the coordinator. Rejecting the last outstanding item can otherwise complete a qualifying assignment and open the payment path for finance.
