# How a full day of portfolio risk review became 10 minutes

Target length: 5-7 minutes. All screen recordings use the synthetic demo at nolangrout.com/demos/portfolio-risk. Never show real Triten data, names or internal screens.

## Hook (0:00-0:30)

ON SCREEN: The demo's question box. Type slowly: "Tell me the top 20 assets I should be worried about, why, and who manages them."

VOICE: This question used to cost a full day of someone's time. At Triten, an employee spent 8+ hours across SharePoint, Monday.com and Yardi to answer it. Now it takes about 10 minutes. Let me show you what changed, and why it is safe.

## Beat one: The old way (0:30-1:30)

ON SCREEN: Simple animation of three boxes labelled "Property system", "Project boards", "Documents", with one person drawing lines between them.

VOICE: Most funds keep financials and leases in one system, project work on boards, and the explanation for everything in documents. A risk review meant opening all three, pulling the numbers, comparing them and then the slowest part: working out who is actually responsible for each asset. And at the end, nobody could easily check the answer.

## Beat two: Governed facts first (1:30-2:45)

ON SCREEN: The three boxes feed into one labelled "Governed facts". Callouts: "source", "date", "who may see it", "conflicts recorded".

VOICE: The first thing I built was not the AI part. It was a governed data platform. It reads Yardi, Monday.com and SharePoint and turns each record into a fact that keeps where it came from, when it was true and who is allowed to see it. When two systems disagree, the conflict is recorded and resolved by written rules. The AI model never decides what is true.

## Beat three: Read-only access for ChatGPT (2:45-3:45)

ON SCREEN: A lock icon over "MCP server: 22 read-only tools". Then an arrow from ChatGPT to the server, and a crossed-out arrow from ChatGPT to the source systems.

VOICE: On top sits a read-only MCP server. MCP is the standard that lets assistants like ChatGPT and Claude call outside tools. This one has 22 tools, and none of them can write. ChatGPT never touches the source systems and has no credentials for them. It can ask. It cannot change anything.

## Beat four: The demo (3:45-5:30)

ON SCREEN: Run the demo. Show the ranked list appearing. Click one asset to expand its reasons, the cited source records and the named managers. Highlight the "synthetic data" label.

VOICE: Here is the question again, on synthetic data. The assistant calls the investigation tools, ranks the exposure, explains why each asset is on the list, cites the records behind it and names the asset and project managers. That last part matters: you end with people to call, not just problems to worry about. And because every figure points back to a source, you can check any line you doubt.

## Beat five: What it means for your fund (5:30-6:15)

ON SCREEN: Three short lines: "Ask whenever you're curious", "Anyone with access can ask", "Every answer shows its work".

VOICE: When a review costs a day, it happens on a schedule. When it costs about 10 minutes, it happens whenever someone is curious. Follow-up questions become normal. And the knowledge stops living in one person's head.
