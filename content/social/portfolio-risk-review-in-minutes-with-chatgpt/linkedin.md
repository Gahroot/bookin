A portfolio risk review used to take a full working day. Now an asset manager asks ChatGPT one question.

"Tell me the top 20 assets I should be worried about, why, and who manages them."

Before: an employee spent 8+ hours across SharePoint, Monday.com and Yardi. Pull the numbers. Read the documents. Weigh one source against another. Then figure out who is actually on each asset.

After: about 10 minutes.

What changed was not the AI model. It was what the model is allowed to touch.

For Triten, I built a governed data platform that reads Yardi, Monday.com and SharePoint and keeps every fact tied to its source, its date and who may see it. On top sits a read-only MCP server with 22 tools. ChatGPT can ask. It cannot change anything.

So the answer comes back ranked, cited, and with the asset managers named. Something you can act on, and something an auditor can trace.

The lesson for fund leaders: connecting ChatGPT to your data is easy. Making its answers trustworthy is the real work.
