DAVE reads existing project evidence and ranks decisions for attention.[^E1]

It brings in project context and keeps track of what I have handled. A quiet queue is also a valid state.[^E1]

The supporting features include project roadmaps, meeting gaps and a delivery record.[^E2][^E3]

For follow-up, I added prepared next actions and a record of recommendations. The evidence documents these capabilities, without a measured operating improvement.[^E1][^E2][^E3][^E4]
