MiMo UltraSpeed runs only on pay-per-token API Credits.[^E2] It is treated as paid and receives the same confirmation and protection as Claude fast mode.[^E2]

MiMo UltraSpeed cannot be saved as a default, restored, or used in chat, by sub-agents, or by goal workers.[^E2]

Startup fallback and compaction summaries skip paid and premium-billed models.[^E2]

Each paid model identifies its standard model.[^E2] When a paid tier is removed, selection falls back to the provider default instead of retaining the paid model.[^E2]

The confirmation text names the actual fallback model instead of saying “standard speed.”[^E2]

The documented outcome is explicit paid-mode confirmation, clearer credit errors, and restrictions on automatic or unattended selection of MiMo UltraSpeed.[^E1][^E2]
