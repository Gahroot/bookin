The deposit distinction is the detail I keep coming back to. Deposits held and deposits required were identified as a reason for outstanding discrepancies.[^E3][^E4]

Before asking someone to make those values agree, ask which question the report is supposed to answer.

Other exceptions were attributed to missing source information and changes to unit areas.[^E3][^E4]

Accuracy here meant representing the source faithfully.[^E2] A nightly dashboard check also compared against source accounting records and an operating list.[^E5]

My takeaway for a COO or fund executive: ask the provider to bring the source report, the delivered output and an explanation of an unresolved difference to the same review.

Make that demonstration part of accepting the system.
