I made faithful representation of the source the acceptance standard.[^E2]

One concrete discrepancy concerned deposits held versus deposits required. The reconciliation identified that distinction as a cause of remaining differences.[^E3][^E4]

Those terms deserve attention before anyone asks for a correction. First decide which measure the report is supposed to represent.

The delivered rent roll matched the source report’s treatment of unit areas, vacant rows and totals.[^E3][^E4]

Other remaining differences were labelled for missing source information and unit-area edits.[^E3][^E4]

I also added a nightly dashboard-accuracy check against source accounting records and an operating list.[^E5]

Before accepting reporting from your team or vendor, ask them to walk through a discrepancy: what differs, why it differs and who has authority to resolve it.
